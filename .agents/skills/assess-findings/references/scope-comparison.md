# Compare Scope Before Selecting Feedback

Use this method when assessing how an implementation covers earlier ideas, proposals or requirements. Work within the requested comparison; a question about one behavior needs only the relevant history and evidence.

## Establish the comparison

Recover the desired user outcomes from the relevant discussion and records. Distinguish an outcome from a suggested mechanism: a later design can replace the mechanism while preserving the outcome. Carry forward accepted, revised, deferred and withdrawn decisions with their sources and reasons. Treat an unclear commitment as unknown.

Extend the existing worklist or inline account with:

- The desired outcome, its source and current standing.
- What the implementation's author commits to cover, with supporting proposal or discussion evidence.
- What is implemented and what the available evidence establishes: observed execution, source-supported behavior or an unverified boundary.
- The remaining difference or uncertainty and the next assessment action.

Reuse primary records and existing coverage rather than maintaining a separate requirements store. Each requested outcome is ready for assessment when its scope and evidence are traceable or the missing source or check is explicit. Apply the main workflow's evidence intake and investigation steps to the remaining questions.

## Derive the feedback

Use the assessed coverage to distinguish current correctness problems, optional enhancements to this change, independent future capabilities and unresolved questions. Explain the expectation basis and practical value behind each recommendation. An author's stated scope is one input: product contracts, misleading output and regressions can establish a defect even without an explicit promise. A broader desired capability can remain valuable beyond this change's scope.

Before presenting a complete comparison, account for every requested outcome and its actual evidence limits. Then select feedback for the requested audience and purpose. A request to send only one suggestion changes the feedback selection; preserve the other supported assessments and deferred opportunities unless the user also revises them. For a feedback-only follow-up, reuse the completed comparison instead of repeating the audit.

Finish when the comparison answers its requested outcomes and the feedback selection follows from that account. Return unknowns and unfinished checks with their limits; the main workflow determines whether further authorized investigation remains.
