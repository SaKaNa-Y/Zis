# Related Work and Discussion

Use this before choosing an independent PR, contribution to existing work, or deferral of a contribution. Work within the consuming task's tracker permissions. The assigned reconciliation owner performs the lookup; Assess judges the returned evidence and requests only missing decisive checks.

## Find related work

Search the configured tracker for the affected scenario, mechanism and known references, including open and closed issues and PRs. Follow links to duplicates, replacements and dependent work when they could change scope or the next action. Explain material exclusions; shared keywords alone do not make work related. Reuse prior searches when their scope and freshness fit this decision. A title, status, or empty search result alone establishes neither resolution nor novelty.

## Read the discussion and recover decisions

For relevant items, inspect the description, applicable implementation changes and discussion. On GitHub, cover issue comments and, for PRs, general comments, review bodies and states, and inline review threads with their replies. These are separate information surfaces; one comments view need not include them all. Follow pagination and check for truncated or inaccessible content. Use the equivalent surfaces on other trackers. Record what was read and any missing coverage rather than treating retrieval success as complete discussion coverage.

Recover what participants accepted, disputed, deferred, withdrew or replaced, with the source and relevant order of replies. Identify scope commitments, unresolved design concerns, ownership, dependencies and waiting conditions. Distinguish a suggestion from an agreed requirement and a participant's view from a maintainer's decision. Check later replies before treating an old objection as current. An approval state, resolved thread or closed item does not by itself explain which concerns were addressed or why work stopped.

Read concrete linked context when needed to interpret those decisions. Do not expand into unrelated repository history. Continue every permitted, available check needed to resolve decision-changing gaps. Complete the inquiry when the relevant discussion surfaces and leads are accounted for. If decisive material cannot be obtained within the current permissions or access, return a bounded assessment with the missing evidence, its owner, the next check and reopening condition; keep the dependent contribution route pending.

## Return the decision basis

Extend the existing assessment or coverage account; do not create a separate tracker inventory. Include:

- Related items and why they matter, with the search scope and material exclusions.
- What the implementation covers, separately from what the discussion decides about remaining work.
- Decisive discussion pointers, their current standing, and unresolved objections, dependencies or waiting conditions.
- Discussion surfaces checked, access or pagination gaps, and the resulting contribution route or pending decision.

Keep the account proportional: a small case can use a few sentences and precise links. When no related work is found, retain the bounded search basis and its limits rather than inventing discussion evidence. A delegated return must carry this basis, not just say that reconciliation passed.

Use this account to distinguish a repair gap from permission or readiness to pursue a separate contribution. Existing work can leave a scenario unimplemented while its discussion still owns the scope or defers the design. Conversely, a closed item or an old objection need not block a different, supported route. Cite the decisions that support the recommendation and identify any inference; do not invent a maintainer requirement. If missing discussion could reverse the route, keep that choice pending while retaining established defect and implementation evidence. Readiness does not authorize publication.
