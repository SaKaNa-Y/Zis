# Prior work and discussion

Use this method to discover opportunities from community feedback or ground a candidate in prior work. Local exploration history comes first, as described in the main workflow. Community discussions can reveal needs, approaches, constraints, and unresolved questions that improve an idea.

## Discover from feedback

When feedback is the starting point, select a corpus or follow concrete leads within the declared scope. Include relevant open and closed work. A requested full review needs an account of the issue population and actual reading depth; otherwise choose a bounded scope that can answer the discovery question. Use the discussion-reading procedure below for decision-changing sources and retain screening limits.

Extract the user's task, conditions, desired outcome, existing alternative and recurring effort or failure. Keep the requested implementation separate. Compare reports by the outcome they share and the conditions that change the answer. Trace original sources: repeated submissions, split issues, quotations and one participant's updates retain their common origin. Independent reports can strengthen demand evidence without establishing prevalence or priority. One concrete case can still justify an opportunity.

Follow repeated workarounds, capabilities lost across integration boundaries, and remaining scenarios after earlier work into a possible additional outcome. Use the main workflow's comparison of alternatives to consider a small convenience, existing capability composition or broader extension where appropriate. Feedback may instead support clearer guidance, simpler behavior, contribution to existing work or ending the lead.

For a common-capability proposal, explain which source outcomes it could cover, which remain separate, and what assumption or exception could change that scope. Supply this evidence to the existing Assess owner for relationship and grouping judgment. Distinct problems can share a useful capability while retaining their acceptance boundaries. Read corrections, closure reasoning and prior scope decisions before treating a recurring symptom as an unmet need.

## Search around the candidate

Search by the user problem and intended capability, including likely synonyms and related implementation terms. Include relevant open, closed, merged, unmerged, and rejected work. A closed item alone does not establish that its proposal was implemented or rejected.

Reuse relevant evidence already gathered in this task or by a co-active skill. Check its scope, date, and remaining gaps before fetching more. Respect any active requirement for an independent audit before tracker research, including delegated research. Once tracker evidence has informed the work, describe subsequent exploration accordingly.

## Read the relevant discussions

Treat search results as leads. For plausible matches and discussions that could change the idea, read the body and complete available discussion: ordinary comments, review summaries, inline threads, and replies where the platform provides them. Include resolved or outdated threads when they explain a decision. Follow links to successor proposals, alternative approaches, or other work that could change the assessment.

Check pagination, truncation, and access limits. Retrieve the remaining pages or linked discussion when available. Record inaccessible or incomplete material as a coverage limit. Expand along concrete leads; a candidate-specific check does not require reading every comment in the repository. Stop when the relevant leads are resolved or their remaining limits are clear.

When status or wording leaves coverage unclear, inspect the relevant change and current public behavior to determine which part shipped and which remains open. Keep proposal, implementation, and observed behavior distinct.

If discussion cannot be fully retrieved, qualify the prior-work conclusion; continue with a supported opportunity when the missing material does not undermine its value or plausible path. Investigate consequential viability gaps before recommending it.

### When using GitHub

[Issue and PR search](https://docs.github.com/en/search-github/searching-on-github/searching-issues-and-pull-requests#search-by-the-title-body-or-comments) includes titles, bodies, and comments by default. Check which [comment types](https://docs.github.com/en/rest/guides/working-with-comments) the chosen tool returns; ordinary conversation comments, review bodies, and inline review threads may need separate retrieval.

## Use what the discussion establishes

For each relevant source, distinguish a proposal for the same capability, supporting work, and feedback about a particular approach. Retain its status, scope, reasoning, and source link. A maintainer rejecting one UI design does not by itself reject every way to meet the underlying need.

Use concrete material to refine the opportunity: a reported user task, a reusable method, an implementation constraint, an alternative, or an unresolved question. State what comes from earlier work and the additional user outcome the revised idea could provide. Preserve the difference between a participant's suggestion, a maintainer decision, and evidence of current behavior. Describe demand and benefits only as strongly as the sources support.

## Match the refined idea and record the result

If the discussion changes the problem, capability, or scope, match the revised opportunity against local records and community work before presenting it. Reuse evidence that still applies and investigate the new gaps. A different title, UI entry point, or combination of existing proposals does not by itself establish an additional capability. Apply the local and community outcome branches in the main workflow.

A material contribution can be new independent demand evidence, a supported relationship, a remaining outcome or a consolidation recommendation. Update a matched primary record when its evidence or judgment changes under the working-record contract. An external issue match does not by itself require a new local opportunity record; use the existing eligibility and identity rules. Preserve pure duplication as a coverage result. Where an opportunity record exists, retain its borrowed approach and incremental outcome there.

Save the contribution, source evidence and relationships through the [exploration checkpoint](exploration-coverage.md#save-and-resume), using that reference's batch fields and readback checks. State a negative result within the checked scope.
