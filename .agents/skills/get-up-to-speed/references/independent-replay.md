# Independent Reader Replay

Use for a delivered path whose setup or hidden author knowledge could prevent a reader from reaching the promised result.

The author remains responsible for research, teaching scope, examples and corrections. Give a replay worker the guide and delivered examples, the learner's declared background, stated prerequisites, permitted isolated workspace and effects, and the outcome to reach. Keep the author's research conversation and unrecorded fixes out of the replay. Do not hide prerequisites that are explicitly available to the intended reader.

The worker follows the delivered instructions in a fresh isolated environment where practical, using the artifacts as delivered. It records the first failed or ambiguous step, exact action, actual result, missing prerequisite and evidence, then continues unaffected paths when meaningful. It may ask for missing information but must record that the guide did not supply it. It returns replayed coverage and limitations rather than silently fixing files and reporting the repaired result as a pass. It does not rewrite the guide, broaden the curriculum or spawn another team.

The author distinguishes guide/example defects from infrastructure limitations and revises the affected material. Replay affected steps after changes; a corrected-session replay is not a fresh independent trial. Report the artifact revision, actual results and untested paths. If an environment prerequisite is unavailable, retain that limit; do not imply an independent run occurred.

Use one responsible replay owner for related follow-ups. Parallelize only genuinely independent paths with separate resource state and proportionate cost. On interruption, stop execution, collect available evidence and preserve or clean owned resources. The author integrates findings before delivery and retains the main skill's completion boundary when missing evidence prevents the promised outcome.
