# Candidate Evidence Assignments

Use after fixing the decision context, Current Baseline and category gates, when independent evidence research merits delegation. The coordinator keeps one candidate index and the workflow's overall saturation and finalist limits.

Assign either a candidate or a comparable question across candidates. For example, one worker can investigate export and exit constraints across several services under the same conditions. Supply the repository need, baseline, hard gates, relevant profiles, candidate identity, requested facts, source hierarchy, permitted read-only checks and stopping condition. Give every worker the same applicable constraints; do not let each invent a scoring system.

Workers return facts and sources, relevant versions/dates, repository-specific inference, counterevidence, unknowns and search coverage. A missing source is an unknown rather than proof a capability is absent. Conflicting source evidence returns for resolution. The assignment permits research and existing Read-only Validation only; installation, new prototypes, service changes and purchasing remain outside its authority. Workers do not publish recommendations on the coordinator's behalf or spawn additional searches.

The coordinator checks that evidence is comparable before judging fit. Resolve decision-changing gaps, reuse returned facts across candidates and preserve missing evidence visibly. Existing solutions and keeping the baseline remain eligible. Only the coordinator assigns final dispositions and adoption paths under the main skill; this workflow does not delegate its judgment to Assess Findings.

Stop new research at the global evidence boundary and collect useful in-flight results. If constraints change, notify only affected owners and invalidate affected comparisons before using their conclusions. Retain a checkpoint on interruption and stop active work. Report unavailable delegation honestly; perform bounded research inline when feasible rather than simulating a team.
