# Zis

Zis is a private publication. Readers and authors sign in with an issued passphrase. The home page and directory show published Articles to both roles.

**Article** is a standalone piece at a permanent address. It has a title, standfirst, publication date and sections. A saved draft is visible only to the author. Publishing edits replaces the public-facing revision at the same private address.

**Article reference** identifies a canonical Link cited by an Article. A citation occurrence attaches a particular passage to that reference and can carry a verbatim excerpt. Occurrences that cite the same Link can quote different passages. Published evidence survives collection expiry.

**Source** is a configured X account or blog index. The browser operator records original material from enabled Sources. **Information Cards** group the text, original post addresses and temporary attachments from a collection. Source configuration persists when cards expire.

**Publication attempt** records one execution of the writer, including its stages, result Articles or reason for finishing without publication. A retry points to the previous attempt. Attempt history expires after seven days. The Article and its permanent identity survive.

**Author** is the owner credential. Author access includes editing Articles, temporary collection material, publication history, legacy bookmarks and storage settings. The **reader** credential allows private reading until revoked.

**Legacy bookmark** preserves the author's saved title, available text or summary, original Link and save time from the former Signal graph.

[ADR 0027](docs/adr/0027-private-article-publication.md) describes the cutover from the former Brief and Information Card reading interfaces.
