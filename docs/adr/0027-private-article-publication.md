# Private Articles replace the Brief archive and card feed

Readers need a permanent article that explains its evidence after the underlying capture expires. The owner also needs to distinguish an empty publication attempt from a failed or missing attempt.

The application keeps the original capture writer and its configured X and blog Sources from ADR 0024. Only authors may access the collection workspace or original capture images. Published Articles take over the reader home and directory. Canonical Links retain their identities under ADR 0001. Article references and occurrence-level excerpts store text independently of collection and credentials. Publishing stores no publisher HTML, consistent with ADR 0005.

This supersedes the archive routes in ADR 0024, the Brief and Signal reading flows, RSS ingestion, content translation and the content-language preference in ADR 0022. Interface language remains a preference. Bookmarks must reconcile before their previous Signal dependencies are removed. Neither release builds nor cleanup jobs apply the cutover migration.

Capture material and publication attempts expire after seven days. The minimal capture receipts described in ADR 0023 continue to prevent ingestion of expired originals. Published Articles, their references, and the owner's migrated bookmarks remain available.
